## How it started

Juniper Street Bakery opened in 2014 with one oven, two bakers and a sourdough starter we still feed every morning.

## What we care about

- Flour from farms within a day's drive
- Slow rises instead of shortcuts
- Nothing wasted: what we don't sell goes to the food bank at closing time

Come and say hello. The first coffee of the day is on us.
